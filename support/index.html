Help & Support

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Find answers to common questions about Pafly below. If you need more help, reach out to us at the bottom.

How Pafly Works

What is Pafly?

Pafly is an anonymous message exchange app. You can write a short message each day, and it gets delivered to a stranger. You may also receive messages from others at any time — like finding a bottle on the shore. No identities are ever revealed.

Why does delivery take time?

The delay is intentional. Receiving a message from a stranger should feel like a surprise, not an instant chat.

Can I write more than one message per day?

No. Each user can write one message per day. This limitation is by design — it makes each message more meaningful and encourages thoughtful writing.

What if no one receives my message?

Messages are matched with available recipients. If no one is available immediately, your message waits in the queue.

Messages

How long can my message be?

Messages can be up to 500 characters. A minimum of 50 characters is required.

Why was my message blocked?

Every message goes through AI-powered content moderation before delivery. Messages containing hate speech, violence, harassment, or illegal content are blocked.

Can I see who sent me a message?

No. Anonymity is core to Pafly. You will never know who wrote a message you received, and no one will ever know you wrote yours unless you choose to reveal your identity deliberately.

Can I reply to a message I received?

Yes. You can send a short text reply (up to 280 characters) to any received message. One reply per message is allowed — there is no back-and-forth conversation.

Reactions

What reactions are available?

Six reactions are available: Love ❤️, So funny 😂, Moving 😢, Resonates 🤔, Hug 🤗 and Beautiful ✨. You can also report a message, but reporting replaces the reaction.

Can I change my reaction?

Only one reaction per message is allowed. Once you react, you cannot change it. Choose the one that best reflects how the message made you feel.

Will the author know I reacted?

The author is notified that someone reacted to their message and can see the reaction type, but they will never know who reacted.

Moderation

How does moderation work?

Messages are reviewed by an AI moderation system before delivery. This filters harmful content like hate speech, violence, and harassment. No human reads your messages during this process.

Account

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can use Pafly immediately with an anonymous account. No email, no password, no personal information required.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Go to your profile and tap "Delete Account" at the bottom. This permanently removes all your personal data. This action cannot be undone.

Notifications

What notifications will I receive?

You can receive notifications for: daily writing reminders, new messages received, reactions on your messages, and replies. Each can be toggled individually in your profile settings.

How do I change notification settings?

Go to your profile, tap "Notifications" in the Settings section. You can enable or disable each notification type independently.
